# Privacy Policy

## Introduction

TestCompany ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **Name and Contact Details**: Full name, email address, phone number
- **Account Information**: Username, password, authentication credentials
- **Demographic Information**: Age, gender, location (when provided)
- **Payment Information**: Billing address, payment method details

### Usage Information
- **Service Usage**: Features accessed, time spent, actions taken
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, browser information
- **Location Data**: IP address, approximate geographic location
- **Interaction Data**: Clicks, navigation paths, search queries

### Automatically Collected Information
- **Log Data**: Server access logs, error reports, performance data
- **Cookies and Tracking**: Session identifiers, preferences, analytics data
- **Communication Data**: Support requests, feedback, survey responses

## How We Use Your Information

### Primary Purposes
- **Service Provision**: Deliver and maintain our services
- **Account Management**: Create and manage user accounts
- **Customer Support**: Provide technical assistance and support
- **Security**: Protect against fraud and security threats

### Secondary Purposes
- **Improvement**: Analyze usage patterns to improve services
- **Communication**: Send important updates and service notices
- **Marketing**: With consent, send promotional materials
- **Analytics**: Generate aggregated, anonymized usage statistics

## Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

### Consent
- Direct marketing communications
- Optional features and services
- Research participation

### Contractual Necessity
- Service provision and account management
- Payment processing
- Customer support

### Legal Obligation
- Compliance with applicable laws
- Regulatory reporting requirements
- Legal proceedings

### Legitimate Interests
- Security and fraud prevention
- Network and service security
- Business analytics and improvement

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

### We Do Not Sell Your Personal Information

We never sell your personal information to third parties.

### When We Share Information
- **Service Providers**: Only when necessary for service delivery
- **Legal Requirements**: When required by law or legal process
- **Business Transfers**: In connection with mergers or acquisitions
- **Safety**: To protect rights, property, or personal safety

### International Data Transfers
- We ensure adequate protection for international transfers
- Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) for EEA transfers
- Privacy Shield certifications where applicable
- Your consent may be required for some transfers

## Your Rights

### Right to Access
- Request copies of your personal information
- Verify what information we hold about you
- Confirm processing activities and purposes

### Right to Rectification
- Correct inaccurate personal information
- Complete incomplete personal information
- Update outdated or incorrect information

### Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")
- Delete personal information when no longer needed
- Remove information processed unlawfully
- Withdraw consent for continued processing

### Right to Restrict Processing
- Limit processing to specific purposes
- Suspend processing during disputes
- Maintain information for legal claims

### Right to Data Portability
- Receive information in structured, machine-readable format
- Transfer data to other service providers
- Request direct transmission between providers

### Right to Object
- Object to processing based on legitimate interests
- Opt out of marketing communications
- Request restriction of direct marketing

### Rights Related to Automated Decision Making
- Information about automated processing
- Human intervention rights
- Express your point of view
- Contest automated decisions

## Data Security

### Security Measures
- **Encryption**: Data encryption in transit and at rest
- **Access Controls**: Strict access limitation and authentication
- **Monitoring**: Regular security monitoring and testing
- **Training**: Employee privacy and security training

### Data Retention
- Retain information only as long as necessary
- Delete or anonymize expired information
- Maintain records for legal and regulatory requirements

## Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under:
- **13 years** (COPPA in the United States)
- **16 years** (GDPR in the European Union)

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware of collecting information from children, we will delete it promptly.
