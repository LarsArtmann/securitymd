# Incident Response Plan

## Purpose

This document outlines the incident response procedures for TestCompany to ensure timely and effective handling of security incidents.

## Incident Definition

A security incident is any unauthorized attempt, successful or not, to access, use, disclose, modify, or destroy information.

## Incident Classification

### Critical Incidents
- System compromise or data breach
- Ransomware or destructive malware
- Widespread service disruption
- Legal or regulatory notification required

### High Incidents
- Significant unauthorized access
- Major service degradation
- Limited data exposure
- External media attention

### Medium Incidents
- Isolated unauthorized access
- Minor service disruption
- Potential policy violations
- Limited business impact

### Low Incidents
- Policy violations without impact
- Minor configuration issues
- Informational security events

## Response Team

### Incident Response Team (IRT)
- **Incident Commander**: Overall coordination
- **Technical Lead**: Investigation and containment
- **Communications Lead**: External communications
- **Legal Counsel**: Legal and regulatory guidance
- **Business Representative**: Business impact assessment

### Escalation Contacts
- **CISO**: security@testcompany.com
- **Executive Team**: Available for critical incidents
- **External Experts**: Retained for specialized incidents

## Response Process

### 1. Detection and Identification
**Timeline**: Immediate upon detection

**Activities**:
- Monitor security alerts and logs
- Review user reports of suspicious activity
- Analyze automated security notifications
- Correlate events across systems

**Response**:
- Create incident ticket
- Classify incident severity
- Notify initial responders

### 2. Initial Assessment
**Timeline**: Within 1 hour of detection

**Activities**:
- Verify incident authenticity
- Assess potential impact
- Determine scope of compromise
- Identify affected systems and data

**Response**:
- Update incident classification
- Notify additional team members as needed
- Begin documentation timeline

### 3. Containment
**Timeline**: Within 2-4 hours based on severity

**Activities**:
- Isolate affected systems
- Block malicious network traffic
- Change compromised credentials
- Implement temporary controls

**Response**:
- Verify containment effectiveness
- Monitor for additional indicators
- Document containment actions

### 4. Investigation and Analysis
**Timeline**: 4-24 hours based on complexity

**Activities**:
- Preserve digital evidence
- Analyze attack vectors
- Determine root cause
- Assess data impact

**Response**:
- Document findings
- Update containment strategy
- Plan eradication activities

### 5. Eradication
**Timeline**: Within 48 hours

**Activities**:
- Remove malware and backdoors
- Patch vulnerable systems
- Rebuild compromised systems
- Update security controls

**Response**:
- Verify eradication effectiveness
- Update detection rules
- Plan recovery activities

### 6. Recovery
**Timeline**: 24-72 hours based on impact

**Activities**:
- Restore systems from clean backups
- Validate system functionality
- Monitor for recurrence
-恢复正常业务运营

**Response**:
- Confirm service restoration
- Update monitoring
- Begin post-incident analysis

### 7. Post-Incident Activities
**Timeline**: Within 1 week

**Activities**:
- Conduct lessons learned analysis
- Update incident response procedures
- Improve security controls
- Report to management

**Response**:
- Document all findings
- Update security policies
- Schedule additional training

## Communications

- Mean time to respond (MTTR)

- Technical skill development
- Process and procedure training
- Cross-team coordination exercises

### General Employees

- Phishing simulation exercises
- Incident reporting procedures
- Basic security hygiene
